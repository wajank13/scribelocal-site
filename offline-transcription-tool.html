Why use ScribeItLocal?

ScribeItLocal is designed for business users, consultants, creators, and professionals who want transcription without sending files to a cloud service. It uses AI transcription locally in your browser, so your video and audio files stay on your device instead of being uploaded to a transcription server.

Private by design

Your media and transcripts are processed locally. ScribeItLocal does not receive your files or transcript text.

Simple workflow

Drop in files, choose a model, and download timestamped Markdown transcripts when processing is complete.

AI Local focus

The tool is part of a broader ScribeItLocal direction: practical AI tools that run locally in your browser.

How to transcribe

  1. Open ScribeItLocal in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Let the selected AI model download and cache locally the first time.
  3. Add files whenever you need transcripts.
  4. Process files locally and save the results to your computer.

Common use cases

  • Work with sensitive meeting recordings.
  • Avoid uploading client or internal business files.
  • Create transcripts even when you want a local-first workflow.
