Why use ScribeItLocal?

ScribeItLocal is designed for creators, marketers, trainers, and business teams who need meeting clips, demos, webinars, or social videos converted to text. It uses AI transcription locally in your browser, so your MP4 stay on your device instead of being uploaded to a transcription server.

Private by design

Your media and transcripts are processed locally. ScribeItLocal does not receive your files or transcript text.

Simple workflow

Drop in files, choose a model, and download timestamped Markdown transcripts when processing is complete.

AI Local focus

The tool is part of a broader ScribeItLocal direction: practical AI tools that run locally in your browser.

How to transcribe

  1. Open the ScribeItLocal transcription tool.
  2. Add your MP4 video or drag it into the drop zone.
  3. Choose the recommended WebGPU model in Chrome or Edge.
  4. Download the Markdown transcript when processing finishes.

Common use cases

  • Repurpose webinar videos into blog drafts or notes.
  • Transcribe training videos without uploading internal content.
  • Create searchable notes from recorded demos or presentations.
