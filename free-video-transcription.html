Why use ScribeItLocal?

ScribeItLocal is designed for content creators, small businesses, educators, and teams that need quick transcripts without subscription friction. It uses AI transcription locally in your browser, so your MP4, MOV, MKV, AVI, and WEBM stay on your device instead of being uploaded to a transcription server.

Private by design

Your media and transcripts are processed locally. ScribeItLocal does not receive your files or transcript text.

Simple workflow

Drop in files, choose a model, and download timestamped Markdown transcripts when processing is complete.

AI Local focus

The tool is part of a broader ScribeItLocal direction: practical AI tools that run locally in your browser.

How to transcribe

  1. Open the transcription tool.
  2. Drop in your video file or batch of files.
  3. Use the recommended GPU model for the fastest experience.
  4. Download your transcript as a Markdown file.

Common use cases

  • Create captions drafts from videos.
  • Extract notes from recorded lessons or demos.
  • Convert video content into searchable text.
