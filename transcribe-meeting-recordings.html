Why use ScribeItLocal for meeting recordings?

ScribeItLocal is designed for business users, consultants, and teams who need to transcribe recorded meetings without sending sensitive audio or video files to a third-party service. It uses AI transcription locally in your browser, so your meeting recordings stay on your device instead of being uploaded to a transcription server.

Protect sensitive conversations

Meeting recordings often contain customer details, strategy, financial context, and internal decisions. ScribeItLocal keeps files on your device.

Unlock tribal knowledge

Turn recorded meetings, interviews, training sessions, and operational calls into searchable text you can reference, share, or feed into AI workflows.

AI-ready transcripts

Generate clean Markdown transcripts with timestamps that can support summaries, documentation, RAG pipelines, knowledge bases, and AI agent workflows.

How to transcribe meeting recordings

  1. Open the free ScribeItLocal tool.
  2. Add your meeting audio or video recording.
  3. Start local AI transcription.
  4. Download the transcript as clean Markdown.

Common use cases

Meeting transcription without the upload risk

Most meeting transcription services require you to upload recordings to their servers. For audio and video that contains confidential business information, customer data, or unreleased strategy, this creates compliance and governance risk. ScribeItLocal's transcription runs entirely in your browser — the audio never leaves your computer.
