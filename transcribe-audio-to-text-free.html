Why use ScribeItLocal?

ScribeItLocal is designed for podcasters, researchers, consultants, and business users who need private audio transcription without creating an account. It uses AI transcription locally in your browser, so your MP3, WAV, and M4A stay on your device instead of being uploaded to a transcription server.

Private by design

Your media and transcripts are processed locally. ScribeItLocal does not receive your files or transcript text.

Simple workflow

Drop in files, choose a model, and download timestamped Markdown transcripts when processing is complete.

AI Local focus

The tool is part of a broader ScribeItLocal direction: practical AI tools that run locally in your browser.

How to transcribe

  1. Open the free transcription tool.
  2. Add one audio file or a batch of files.
  3. Let the local Whisper model generate the transcript.
  4. Save or download the finished Markdown files.

Common use cases

  • Transcribe podcast drafts and voice notes.
  • Convert customer call recordings for internal review.
  • Create written notes from interviews or research conversations.
