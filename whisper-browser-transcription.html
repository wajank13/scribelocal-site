Why use ScribeItLocal?

ScribeItLocal is designed for AI-curious users, developers, creators, and business teams who want a simple local transcription interface. It uses AI transcription locally in your browser, so your common video and audio formats stay on your device instead of being uploaded to a transcription server.

Private by design

Your media and transcripts are processed locally. ScribeItLocal does not receive your files or transcript text.

Simple workflow

Drop in files, choose a model, and download timestamped Markdown transcripts when processing is complete.

AI Local focus

The tool is part of a broader ScribeItLocal direction: practical AI tools that run locally in your browser.

How to transcribe

  1. Open ScribeItLocal in a modern browser.
  2. Choose a Whisper model option.
  3. Add your files and start transcription.
  4. Review and download the generated transcript.

Common use cases

  • Try local AI transcription without installing Python.
  • Transcribe batches of files from a simple web UI.
  • Keep files local while using modern AI models.
