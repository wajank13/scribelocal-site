ScribeItLocal uses OpenAI's Whisper model via the Transformers.js library to transcribe audio and video entirely within your browser. No audio data is transmitted to any server. Model weights are downloaded from Hugging Face and cached locally in your browser. This tool is provided free of charge — supported by unobtrusive ads.
