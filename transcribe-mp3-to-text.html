Why use ScribeItLocal?

ScribeItLocal is designed for podcasters, students, consultants, and creators with interviews, notes, or audio drafts. It uses AI transcription locally in your browser, so your MP3 stay on your device instead of being uploaded to a transcription server.

Private by design

Your media and transcripts are processed locally. ScribeItLocal does not receive your files or transcript text.

Simple workflow

Drop in files, choose a model, and download timestamped Markdown transcripts when processing is complete.

AI Local focus

The tool is part of a broader ScribeItLocal direction: practical AI tools that run locally in your browser.

How to transcribe

  1. Open the free ScribeItLocal tool.
  2. Add your MP3 file.
  3. Start local AI transcription.
  4. Download the finished transcript.
